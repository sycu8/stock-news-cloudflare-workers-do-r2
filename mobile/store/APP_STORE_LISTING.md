## Description

Stock News by Orange Cloud brings together Vietnam stock market headlines, watchlists, and investor tools in one mobile app.

Features:
• Daily news aggregation from trusted RSS and public sources
• Filters by date, source, keyword, and sentiment
• Personal watchlist (up to 18 symbols)
• Investor Desk: morning brief, Fear/Greed index, sector rotation
• Reference charts: VN-Index, FX, gold
• Optional Telegram alerts

Important: Content is for informational purposes only and is not investment advice. Market data may be delayed or inaccurate.
