# App Store — App Privacy questionnaire

## Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?
**Yes**

## Data linked to the user

| Data type | Used for | Linked to identity |
|-----------|----------|-------------------|
| Product interaction (anonymous page metrics) | Analytics | No |
| Other data (watchlist symbols, optional Telegram chat ID) | App functionality, notifications | Only if user enables Telegram |

## Data not collected

- Contact info (unless user emails support outside the app)
- Health & fitness
- Financial info (payment, credit — **not collected**)
- Location
- Sensitive info
- Contacts
- Browsing history (beyond in-app navigation)
- Search history (queries processed server-side, not sold)

## Tracking
**No** — we do not use data for cross-app tracking or advertising. No IDFA usage.

## Third-party SDKs in native shell

Capacitor core plugins only (App, Browser, Network, SplashScreen, StatusBar). No ad SDKs.
